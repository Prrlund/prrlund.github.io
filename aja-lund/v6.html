“Throwing myself between the true and the imagined, the dead and the living.”

Aja Lund — Artist statement

Born in the woods.
Keeps everything.

Born in a small solitary village in the woods on the west coast of Finland. When Aja was twelve, her father died — and collecting became survival: objects as containers of memories and emotions.

Since then she has gathered things, people and impressions. Under the skin. Close to the heart. Master of Culture and Arts in Photography, a year in Paris with Margot Wallard & JH Engström, internship with Anders Petersen — and a poetry prize on the side.
