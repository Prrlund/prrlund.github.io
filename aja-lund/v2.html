Aja Lund

Born in the woods.
Collecting ever since.

My roots are deeply anchored in a cultural heritage of hard work and religious conviction. I was born in a small solitary village in the woods on the west coast of Finland in the late 70's.

When I was twelve, my father died. As a child I had always collected things, but then it became even more important — objects as containers of memories and emotions. I have continued to gather things, people and impressions near me. Under the skin. Close to the heart.

Aja Lund holds a Master of Culture and Arts in Photography (Novia UAS), studied a year at Atelier Smedsby in Paris under Margot Wallard and JH Engström, and interned with Anders Petersen in Stockholm. She teaches photography at Novia UAS and founded Kulturkollektivet Insidan.
