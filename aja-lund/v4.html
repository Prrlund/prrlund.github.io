Profile The girl from the woods who kept everything

My roots are deeply anchored in a cultural heritage of hard work and religious conviction. I was born in a small solitary village in the woods on the west coast of Finland in the late 70's. When I was twelve, my father died. As a child I had always collected things, but then it became even more important for me to collect objects — containers of memories and emotions.

And not just objects: I wanted to collect and embrace life fully, through my eyes and documentation. I have continued to gather both things, people and impressions near me. Under the skin. Close to the heart. To encapsulate, maintain and care for.

Today Aja Lund works as a fine art and documentary photographer in Jakobstad — exhibiting across Finland, Sweden and Iceland, publishing photo books, teaching photography at Novia UAS, and running Kulturkollektivet Insidan, a home for cultural workers and conversations.
