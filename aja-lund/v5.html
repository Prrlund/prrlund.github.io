My roots are deeply anchored in a cultural heritage of hard work and religious conviction. I was born in a small solitary village in the woods on the west coast of Finland in the late 70's. When I was twelve, my father died. As a child I had always collected things, but then it became even more important for me to collect objects — containers of memories and emotions.

I have continued to gather both things, people and impressions near me. To encapsulate, maintain and care for. I am drawn to the contrasts, throwing myself between the true and the imagined, the ruled and the free, the thought and the intuitive, the dead and the living.
