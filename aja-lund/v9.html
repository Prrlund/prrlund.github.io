The keeper of the cabinet

Born among woods,
raised among things

My roots are deeply anchored in a cultural heritage of hard work and religious conviction. I was born in a small solitary village in the woods on the west coast of Finland in the late 70's. When I was twelve, my father died — and collecting became even more important: objects as containers of memories and emotions.

Today the cabinet holds twenty years of photographs, two books, a poetry prize and a teaching desk at Novia UAS — and it keeps growing. Because a collector always wants more.
