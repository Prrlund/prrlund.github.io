About

A collector who happens to photograph — or the other way around.

Born in a small solitary village in the woods on the west coast of Finland. When Aja was twelve her father died, and collecting became a way of keeping: objects as containers of memories and emotions. The camera followed naturally — another way to gather life, picture by picture.

She holds an MA in Photography (Novia UAS), spent a year at Atelier Smedsby in Paris under Margot Wallard and JH Engström, and interned with Anders Petersen in Stockholm. She teaches photography, runs Kulturkollektivet Insidan, and won the Solveig von Schoultz poetry prize in 2024.
