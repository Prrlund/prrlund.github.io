Jag är en samlare, jag vill ha mer!

Subject

Aja Lund, b. 1979

Origin

A solitary village in the woods, west coast of Finland

Occupation

Collecting memories — on film and in boxes

Status

Wants more

As a child I had always collected things. When I was twelve, my father died — and it became even more important for me to collect objects: containers of memories and emotions. And not just objects. I wanted to collect and embrace life fully, through my eyes and documentation.

I have continued to gather both things, people and impressions near me. Under the skin. Close to the heart. To encapsulate, maintain and care for.

This archive holds eight series — each one a drawer in the cabinet. Open them carefully.
