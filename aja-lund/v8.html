Chapter IObjectsp. 12
ObjectsPlate 1 — studio typology

As a child I had always collected things. When I was twelve, my father died — and it became even more important for me to collect objects: containers of memories and emotions. Arranged on white, photographed one collection at a time, they become inventories of a life.

Not just objects: I wanted to collect and embrace life fully, through my eyes and documentation. To encapsulate, maintain and care for. View the series →

Chapter IICollectorsp. 38
CollectorsPlate 2 — documentary

I have continued to gather both things, people and impressions near me. In this series I meet the others — the fellow gatherers, the keepers of piles and treasures — and photograph the lives their collections carry.

The series became the book Jag är en samlare, jag vill ha mer! (2019), nominated for Book of the Year at the Collector's Awards in Stockholm. View the series →

Chapter IIIHumansp. 64
HumansPlate 3 — portraits

To watch. To identify. To be with. To be more. Portraits made close — under the skin, close to the heart — searching for the being, the human being.

Trained in the tradition of Anders Petersen, with whom Aja interned in Stockholm, and JH Engström and Margot Wallard, under whom she studied a year at Atelier Smedsby in Paris. View the series →

Chapter IVLandscapesp. 92
Levande och dödaPlate 4 — landscapes

The living and the dead. Born in a small solitary village in the woods on the west coast of Finland, Aja returns to the snow, the pines and the silence — performing and exploring the extremes of the outskirts to wake something up.

Catch, release, awake more. View the series →

“Share the secrets of life, picture by picture, piece by piece — but never completely. Because completely does not exist.”
