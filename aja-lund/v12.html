my story, shortly —

It began in a village in the woods.

I was born on the west coast of Finland in the late 70's, in a small solitary village anchored in hard work and conviction. When I was twelve, my father died — and collecting became my way of keeping: objects as containers of memories and emotions.

Since then I've gathered things, people and impressions. I studied photography (MA, Novia), spent a year in Paris with Margot Wallard and JH Engström, learned from Anders Petersen in Stockholm — and somewhere along the way raised five children, founded a culture collective and wrote prize-winning poetry.
